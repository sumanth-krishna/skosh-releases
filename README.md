# Skosh for Mac

Installers for Skosh for Mac. Download the newest: https://www.getskosh.com/download/mac

Skosh for Mac runs Sparky, Skosh's recruiting assistant, on your own computer. Nothing reaches a candidate until a person approves it.

Until the app is notarized by Apple, the first time you open it macOS may say it cannot check the app: open System Settings > Privacy & Security & click Open Anyway.

Questions: support@getskosh.com
